# Multi-offset photon and residual-Kerr audit

Ten prospectively defined selection offsets are evaluated per coupling condition. Intervals are empirical 2.5--97.5 percentiles across offsets and are not fabrication-tolerance intervals.

- Reference input: `0.224 nW/ring`.
- Target precision scale: `1e+04` effective photons.
- Kerr boundary: `|K| nbar/kappa = 0.1`.
- Representative `K/(2 pi) = 0.437 Hz/photon`.

- `J=0`: precision power median `13.99 W/ring` [`13`, `14.89`]; Kerr-boundary power median `0.09924 mW/ring` [`0.09924`, `0.09924`]; boundary effective photons median `0.07094` [`0.06667`, `0.07636`]; required signal/Kerr improvement median `1.41e+05` [`1.31e+05`, `1.5e+05`].
- `J=0.65`: precision power median `37.77 W/ring` [`36.51`, `39.45`]; Kerr-boundary power median `1.064 mW/ring` [`1.064`, `1.064`]; boundary effective photons median `0.2818` [`0.2698`, `0.2915`]; required signal/Kerr improvement median `3.548e+04` [`3.43e+04`, `3.707e+04`].
