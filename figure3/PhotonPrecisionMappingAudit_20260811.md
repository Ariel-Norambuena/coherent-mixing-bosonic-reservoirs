# Photon-precision mapping audit

This diagnostic reinterprets the archived validation-only photon-scaled noise sweep. It does not rerun trajectories and is not a receiver measurement.

- Loaded linewidth: `8 MHz`; external coupling fraction: `0.5`.
- Detection efficiency: `0.50`; heterodyne penalty: `3 dB`.
- Integration aperture: `238.7 ps`.
- Mean intracavity photons, J=0: `5.029 +/- 2.26`.
- Mean intracavity photons, J=0.65: `0.4326 +/- 0.137`.
- Power values scale the ideal reference drive of 0.224 nW/ring. They exclude LO, insertion loss, RF/ADC, stabilization, and wall-plug power and must not be quoted as total system energy.
